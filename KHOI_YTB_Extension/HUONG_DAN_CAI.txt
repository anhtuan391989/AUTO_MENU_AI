TIEN ICH "KHOI" - YOUTUBE CHO AUTO MENU AI  (ban 1.4.0)

Tien ich gui ve AUTO MENU AI (ngay tren may ban, khong ra Internet): bai YouTube dang phat, dang chay hay tam dung,
vi tri that (tua la thay ngay), thoi luong, va co dang chieu quang cao khong.

CAI DAT (1 lan, Coc Coc / Chrome):
1. Mo trinh duyet, vao dia chi:  chrome://extensions   (Coc Coc: coccoc://extensions)
2. Bat "Che do danh cho nha phat trien" (Developer mode) o goc tren ben phai.
3. Bam "Tai tien ich da giai nen" (Load unpacked) -> chon thu muc  extensions\KHOI_YTB_Extension  trong thu muc AUTO_MENU_AI.
4. Mo lai AUTO MENU AI, mo 1 bai YouTube, bam phat.

LUU Y:
- Menu chi nhan du lieu tu cong 8765 tren chinh may nay. Neu app HT DAW dang chay cung luc va chiem cong 8765 thi Menu
  van chay binh thuong bang cach cu (SMTC); dong HT DAW la Menu tu nhan lai trong ~15 giay.
- Ban tien ich cu (1.3.1) khong gui "thoi luong" nen Menu tam bo qua no. Phai dung ban 1.4.0 nay.
